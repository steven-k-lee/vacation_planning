🚗 Day 1 (Oct 8): Wiesbaden to Füssen
📍 Wiesbaden 🛣 Road trip 🏁 Fussen
⏰ Logistics: Leave Wiesbaden at 3:00 PM to beat Frankfurt rush hour. Break at 6:30 PM near Ulm. Arrive in Füssen by 7:45 PM.
🏨 Hotel: Villa Fantasia Budget Boutique Hotel (Ottostraße 1, Füssen). Use hotel guest parking or public Parkgarage P5 City.
🍽 Restaurant Recommendations: Traditional town taverns. Order plain Wiener Schnitzel with fries or vegetable-free Kasspatzln (cheese egg noodles with fried onions).
🍴 Restaurant Options (Includes Lunch & Dinner)
Madame Plüsch
⭐ 4.6 €20–60 Bavarian Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Thu–Sun & Mon: 12:00 PM–4:00 PM, 5:00 PM–11:00 PM (Tue–Wed Closed)

Charming, historic old-town tavern featuring beautifully styled rooms filled with 1920s retro decor. Highly celebrated for serving up massive, authentic portions of house specialties like cheese spätzle, tender pork roast, and excellent local beers.

Gasthaus Zum Schwanen
⭐ 4.7 €20–30 Traditional Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Tue–Sat: 12:00 PM–10:00 PM (Sun–Mon Closed)

Quaint and cozy historic inn offering a warm, authentic culinary Bavarian experience. The bustling kitchen cooks incredible regional standouts, ranging from crisp Wiener Schnitzels to savory, refined meat dishes served on a lovely pedestrian terrace.

Beim Ditsch
⭐ 4.7 €10–20 Bavarian Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Tue & Sat: 11:30 AM–8:00 PM, Thu–Fri: 11:30 AM–9:00 PM

Hidden down a quiet historic side street, this warm, family-run gem provides fantastic value for high-quality German comfort food. Visitors rave about their exceptionally crispy schnitzels, hearty beef roulades, and swift, multi-lingual hospitality.

Beim Olivenbauer
⭐ 4.6 €20–30 German/Pizza Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 11:00 AM–10:00 PM

A highly popular spot blending alpine tavern charm with a diverse menu of German classics and wood-oven pizza. Families love the quick service, massive portions, separate gluten-free fryer options, and dedicated indoor children's play room.

Restaurant Ludwigs
⭐ 4.6 €20–30 Regional Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 11:30 AM–10:00 PM

Conveniently situated in the absolute heart of the pedestrian zone, this fast-paced restaurant features a modern Bavarian atmosphere. Offers an extensive family menu highlighting giant pork chops, dynamic custom burgers, and sweet kaiserschmarrn pancakes.

🚠 Day 2 (Oct 9): Crossing to Italy & Seceda
🏔 Fern Pass / Brenner Pass 🛗 Seceda 🏨 Ortisei
🛗 Seceda Cable Car: Runs daily 8:30 AM – 5:30 PM (closes 5:00 PM after Oct 12). Schedules depend on season and weather conditions. Check live updates on the Official Website.
⏰ Logistics: Leave at 8:30 AM via Fern Pass and Brenner Pass in full daylight. Arrive at Seceda terminal in Ortisei by 12:00 PM.
🥾 Walk: Cable car up to 2,500m. The iconic ridge view is a completely flat, wide 10-minute stroll along a gravel service track.
🏨 Hotel: Residence Cesa Sassela (Streda Sobosch 12, Ortisei). Free private parking included.
🍽 Restaurant Recommendations:
· Lunch: Baita Sofie Hütte (plain hot fries/cheese slices at the top).
· Dinner: Ristorante Pizzeria Cascade (plain wood-fired Margherita Pizza or butter pasta).
🍴 Restaurant Options (Includes Lunch & Dinner)
Mauriz Keller
⭐ 4.4 €20–30 Tyrolean/Pizza Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon & Wed–Sun: 12:00 PM–2:00 PM, 5:00 PM–9:30 PM

Tucked beautifully into a historic, rustic cellar tavern lined with wood accents right in the center. Delivers an extensive menu of homemade pastas served in massive iron pans, rich truffle risottos, and thin-crust, wood-fired pizzas.

Cascade Ristorante
⭐ 4.3 €20–30 Italian Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 11:00 AM–11:00 PM (Continuous)

Located just steps from the walking center, this spacious alpine eatery showcases an excellent combination of professional service and local tradition. Perfect for families wanting to sample authentic Tyrolean venison or simple wood-oven classics.

Vedl Mulin
⭐ 4.5 €20–30 Local/Italian Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 12:00 PM–9:30 PM (Continuous)

A highly popular local staple relying heavily on fresh ingredients sourced straight from Val Gardena alpine farmers. Extremely accommodating to families, preparing everything from tender spare ribs and giant schnitzels to basic child-friendly pastas.

Turonda Bistro
⭐ 4.6 €20–30 Gourmet Pizza Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 12:00 PM–10:30 PM (Continuous)

A modern, highly rated bistro specializing in remarkably light, multi-cooked Neapolitan style crusts. The sleek, stylized wooden interior and relaxed environment provide an upscale yet entirely informal dynamic that easily satisfies both parents and kids.

LaBar Ristorante
⭐ 4.6 €20–30 Italian Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Tue–Sun: 12:00 PM–3:00 PM, 5:30 PM–10:30 PM

Situated slightly outside the crowded center in Roncadizza, this woman-owned neighborhood spot is praised for serving up some of the best pizza in the area. Provides quick service, family pricing, and excellent takeaway options for easy dining.

☀ Day 3 (Oct 10): Alpe di Siusi E-Bike Loop
🚲 Compatsch 🗺 Alpe di Siusi Loop 📸 Meadow Trails
⚠️ Warning: You must drive up the mountain access road before the strict 9:00 AM private vehicle gate closure.
⏰ Logistics: Depart apartment at 8:15 AM. Park at Compatsch P2 lot. Rent e-bikes and cruise wide meadow tracks effortlessly from 9:00 AM to 1:30 PM.
🚲 Pre-Book E-Bikes: Book at Alpincenter | Book at Sport Hans
🗺 Maps & Trails: Alpe di Siusi E-Bike Loop Map
🍽 Restaurant Recommendations:
· Lunch: Malga Sanon (meadow terrace, order plain Canederli allo Speck).
· Dinner: L'Ostaria Ortisei (plain meat-and-bun Hamburgers with wedges).
🍴 Restaurant Options (Includes Lunch & Dinner)
Tschötsch Alm
⭐ 4.7 €20–30 Alpine Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Fri & Sun: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM

Perched beautifully on the hiking trails of the Puflatsch slope, this alpine hut features a massive panoramic sun terrace. Excellent for enjoying re-imagined regional classics like speck dumplings and house-made pasta scraps right during your loop.

Huber Schwaige
⭐ 4.9 €20–30 Bio-Alpine Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM

An incredible, top-rated bio-farm hut featuring unmatched meadow views and a direct outdoor petting zoo and playground for kids. Famous for its artisan cheese platters, house-smoked sausages, and exceptional, massive kaiserschmarrn pancakes.

Ristorante Alpi
⭐ 4.4 €10–20 Italian/Pizza Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 12:00 PM–2:30 PM, 7:00 PM–10:00 PM

Perfectly nestled in the central Compatsch hub, this casual guesthouse restaurant offers rapid service and great value. Highly recommended for family sit-down lunches or filling pizza dinners immediately following your mountain cycling excursion.

Alpina Chalet
⭐ 4.4 €10–40 Alpine Grill Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 11:00 AM–10:30 PM

Offering a trendy, mountain-chic chalet atmosphere with magnificent valley backdrops. Outdoor dining tables paired with cozy individual stone fireplaces make it a special spot for parents to enjoy premium local steaks while kids eat fresh artisan sliders.

Almgasthof Mont Seuc
⭐ 4.4 €20–30 Alpine Fusion Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 8:30 AM–6:00 PM

Positioned right at the high mountain cableway station, this establishment serves up legendary unobstructed views. Highly family-friendly, complete with a massive outdoor kids' playground and an excellent terrace for viewing the sunset over the peaks.

🚣 Day 4 (Oct 11): Lake Boats & Tre Cime Towers
🚣 Lago di Braies ⛰️ Tre Cime Towers 🛏️ Dobbiaco
⚠️ Warning: Pre-book your 12-hour high-altitude vehicle entry toll pass (€40) online at pass.auronzo.info before arriving.
⏰ Logistics: Check out at 8:30 AM. Arrive at Lago di Braies at 9:45 AM. Rent a wooden rowboat fits all 5. Walk flat shoreline track.
🥾 Tre Cime Walk: Drive south to Tre Cime at 12:30 PM. Walk Path 101 to Rifugio Lavaredo—a completely wide, flat, gravel road directly underneath giant vertical rock towers.
🗺 Hiking Tracks: Path 101 to Rifugio Lavaredo Trail Map
🏨 Hotel: B&B Hotel Heidi (Gratsch 6, Via Grazze 6, Dobbiaco). Free private parking on-site.
🍽 Restaurant Recommendations:
· Lunch: Baita Langen Hof (beef Gulasch con Canederli in plain gravy).
· Dinner: Snetonstube (creamy bacon egg Gnocchetti Spätzle).
🍴 Restaurant Options (Includes Lunch & Dinner)
Winkelkeller
⭐ 4.5 €30–40 Tyrolean Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 12:00 PM–2:00 PM, 6:00 PM–9:30 PM

A beautifully charming, historic restaurant featuring a cozy fireplace indoors and a lively garden outdoor area. Treats hikers to incredible Tyrolean meat platters, slow-cooked deer goulash, and traditional dumplings prepared three distinct ways.

Schlosskeller
⭐ 4.4 €20–30 Tavern/BBQ Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Tue–Sun: 12:00 PM–3:00 PM, 5:00 PM–11:00 PM

Tucked safely inside an authentic, wood-clad castle cellar tavern, this atmospheric gem provides amazing value. The family-friendly spaces are highly popular for customized grilled meat skewers, local venison, and rich cheese spätzle.

Gasthaus Weber
⭐ 4.5 €20–30 Homestyle Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 12:00 PM–2:00 PM, 6:00 PM–9:00 PM

A quaint, deeply authentic mountain chalet trattoria that identifies as a proud, women-owned establishment. Celebrated for serving simple comfort classics like savory barley soup, polenta with sausage, and fresh apple strudel with heavy cream.

Kugler Speckstube
⭐ 4.7 €30–40 Alpine/Speck Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon & Wed–Sun: 10:00 AM–3:00 PM, 6:00 PM–11:30 PM

Emulating a classic farm cottage, this cozy spot is one of the very few high-altitude places that still cures its own artisan speck and ham. Features grand, welcoming dining halls optimized for family groups traveling with young toddlers.

Hans Pizzeria
⭐ 4.6 €10–20 Pizza/Pasta Lunch & Dinner
🕒 Mon–Sun: 12:00 PM–3:00 PM, 5:30 PM–11:00 PM

Boasting thousands of highly rated reviews, this sleek, modern eatery is entirely lined with beautiful South Tyrolean wood paneling. Offers an expansive, fast menu of standard Italian pizzas, fresh pastas, and local wines that please all ages.
