Day 2 · Sunday, November 1

Bavarian Drives, Castle Views & Twilight Cycling

Open master route
Timing risk: Castle access, Marienbrücke, carriage service, and E-bike availability are weather-dependent. Sunset is around 4:50 PM; confirm the rental return time and bring lights.
+Tickets & reservations0 / 4 done

  1. Choose a one-day car vignette, enter the license plate and registration country carefully, and set November 1 as the validity date.

    Buy from the official ASFINAG shop

  2. Book the November 1 guided tour near 2:30 PM. Save the ticket offline and arrive in Hohenschwangau 1.5–2 hours before admission.

    Buy from the official Ticket Center

  3. Book the Schwangau location at Gipsmühlweg 5 for a 3:45 PM pickup, confirm lights and the return deadline, then save the confirmation.

    Reserve bikes online · Call +49 174 1825489 · Email Bike Füssen

  4. Pay for parking in Hohenschwangau. Pay the driver €8 uphill or €4 downhill and carry euros in cash; service is weather-dependent and cannot be reserved.

    Check official castle access guidance

Marienbrücke has no separate admission fee, but access can close because of weather or maintenance.
