🚗 Day 1 (October 31): Swiss High Alps & Golden Hour

  • 4:00 AM – 9:00 AM | The Early Departure: Leave Wiesbaden down the A5. Purchase Swiss vignette at the Basel border.
  • 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM | Arrival & Bag Drop: Park at Lauterbrunnen Station Garage. Drop bags at Hotel Oberland.
  • 9:30 AM – 10:20 AM | Wengen Postcard Stop: Cogwheel train to Wengen. 5-min walk to the Wengen Swiss Reformed Church for the iconic valley view.
  • 10:24 AM – 12:30 PM | High Alps Stroll & Lunch: Train up to Kleine Scheidegg (2,061m). Stroll to Fallbodensee lake under the Eiger. Lunch at Chaletbar Kleine Scheidegg.
  • 12:30 PM – 2:30 PM | Waterfall Walk: Train back to valley. Flat walk to the misty base of Staubbach Falls.
  • 2:30 PM – 5:30 PM | Sunset Over the Lakes: Drive 20 mins to Interlaken Ost Garage. Funicular up to Harder Kulm. Walk the glass Two Lakes Bridge. Catch final funicular down at 5:00 PM.
  • 6:30 PM onward | The Fondue Feast: Swiss cheese fondue dinner at Restaurant Schützen Lauterbrunnen near your hotel.

🏰 Day 2 (November 1): Bavarian Drives, Castle Views & Twilight Cycling

  • 8:30 AM – 1:00 PM | Cross-Country Alpine Drive: Check out of Hotel Oberland. Drive past Lake Lucerne, crossing into Austria (Bregenz corridor) and onward to Bavaria.
  • 1:00 PM – 1:45 PM | Arrival & Quick Snack: Park at Hohenschwangau Lots. Grab a quick pretzel or bratwurst.
  • 1:45 PM – 2:30 PM | Horse Carriage Ascent: Catch a traditional horse-drawn carriage across from Hotel Müller up the mountain forest slope.
  • 2:30 PM – 3:30 PM | Fairytale Castle Tour: Pre-booked indoor tour of Neuschwanstein Castle. Visit Marienbrücke for photos.
  • 3:45 PM – 5:00 PM | Golden Hour Bike Loop: Carriage/walk down. Grab E-bikes at BIKE FÜSSEN Schwangau. 1-hour twilight loop through meadows and Lake Alpsee shore.
  • 5:15 PM onward | Cozy Tavern Dinner: Check into your hotel near Füssen Altstadt. Dinner at Gasthof Krone Füssen.

🍦 Day 3 (November 2): Royal Palaces, Gelato Dash & Return

  • 9:00 AM – 9:45 AM | Ammergau Alps Drive: Check out and drive through the dramatic mountain valley.
  • 9:45 AM – 10:25 AM | Linderhof Palace Tour: Park and visit Linderhof Palace. Indoor tour at 10:00 AM sharp to see the opulent chambers.
  • 10:25 AM – 11:00 AM | The Gelato Dash: Drive 15 mins to Oberammergau. Visit Eiscafé Paradiso at 10:40 AM opening. Photograph the *Lüftlmalerei* frescoes.
  • 11:00 AM – 3:30 PM | The Homeward Stretch: Hit the A7 north back to Hessen. Arrive in Wiesbaden at 3:30 PM sharp.

🎫 Ticketing & Budget Summary

  • Day 1 Lifts: Buy point-to-point round-trip tickets at kiosks (Lauterbrunnen–Kleine Scheidegg ~CHF 64, Harder Kulm ~CHF 44).
  • Day 2 Neuschwanstein: Pre-book timed-entry tickets weeks in advance online via the official portal.
  • Day 2 Carriages: Cash only (€8.00 uphill / €4.00 downhill).
  • Day 3 Linderhof: Buy at the property ticket booth (~€10.00).
  • Vignettes: Swiss Vignette (CHF 40 at border). Austrian 1-Day Vignette (~€3.40 online).

❄️ Seasonal & Packing Logistics

  • Vehicle: Winter/all-season tires marked with the Alpine symbol are legally required in wintry conditions.
  • Weather & Light: Sunset is ~4:50 PM. Deep valley shadows mean rapid temperature drops. Packing windproof layers, gloves, and thick scarves is vital for the E-bike and palace strolls.
